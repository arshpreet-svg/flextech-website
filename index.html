FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What does Flextech Automation Technologies do?

We manufacture welding fixtures and checking fixtures, design fixtures in our design centre, support line integration, and provide technical consultancy for the 2-wheeler and 4-wheeler automobile industries.

What is the difference between a welding fixture and a checking fixture?

A welding fixture holds and clamps components in the correct position while they are welded, so every assembly comes out the same. A checking fixture is used after manufacturing to quickly verify that a part's profile, holes and critical features match the design.

Do you design fixtures in-house?

Yes. Our design centre develops welding and checking fixture designs from concept to detailed manufacturing drawings.

Which industries do you work with?

We work with 2-wheeler and 4-wheeler automobile manufacturers and their component suppliers.

What does your technical consultancy cover?

Fixture concept and design review, welding process and quality improvement, inspection strategy, and productivity improvement on your production line.
