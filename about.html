About Company

Your Partner in Fixture Engineering

We specialize in the manufacturing of high-quality welding fixtures and checking fixtures, alongside providing expert technical consultancy tailored for both 2-wheeler and 4-wheeler automobile industries.

We work closely with our clients to understand their parts, processes and quality requirements, and deliver fixtures that fit seamlessly into their production lines.

Our commitment is to drive manufacturing excellence, accuracy, and efficiency for our clients.
